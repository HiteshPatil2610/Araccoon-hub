# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Araccoon Hub to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour includes:

- being kind, patient and respectful of differing opinions and experience;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing other people's private information, such as an address or email, without their explicit permission;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub: open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/HiteshPatil2610/Araccoon-hub/security/advisories/new) and say it is a conduct report, or contact [@HiteshPatil2610](https://github.com/HiteshPatil2610) directly. Every report will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Depending on the seriousness, the maintainer may respond with a private warning, a request for a public apology, a temporary ban from interacting with the project, or a permanent ban.
