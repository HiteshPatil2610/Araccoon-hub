# Araccoon promo video

`index.html` is the animated promo; `render.mjs` records it to `araccoon-promo.mp4` with headless Chrome (`CHROME_PATH` overrides the browser).

The page loads its styles and components from `design-system/`, which is **not included in the repository** (it is kept private). Without it the page will not render, but the finished video `araccoon-promo.mp4` is included. The video and `assets/` are artwork under [CC BY-NC 4.0](../../LICENSE-ART.md).
