## What counts as artwork

- `art/`: the 3D model, textures, renders, posters and the scripts' image outputs
- `public/art/` and `public/raccoon-mascot.jpg`
- `video/araccoon-promo/assets/` and `video/araccoon-promo/araccoon-promo.mp4`
- Any other image, video or 3D file that shows the Araccoon mascot

Scripts that *generate* artwork (for example `art/posters/*.py` or
`art/araccoon/scripts/*.py`) are code, so they are MIT licensed. The images they
produce are artwork.

## What you may do

- Share and adapt the artwork, as long as you **credit "Araccoon by Hitesh Patil"**
  and link to this repository and the license.
- Use it in personal, educational and other non-commercial projects.

## What needs permission

- Any **commercial use** of the artwork: selling prints or merchandise, using the
  mascot in a product, a paid service, an advertisement, or a store.
- Using the Araccoon name or mascot as the brand or logo of your own project or
  storefront. If you fork this project to run your own store, replace the mascot
  and the name with your own.

To ask for permission, open an issue in this repository.
