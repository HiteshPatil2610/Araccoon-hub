Raccoon Hub is a small internet treasure map of clever objects and useful oddities. Every find on the site is picked by hand, one at a time, and opens on Amazon, where prices and availability are set.

## What you'll find here

These articles go a little deeper than the product cards:

- **Roundups** of finds that work well together, like a better desk or a calmer bedroom.
- **Notes from use**: what a find is good at, and where it falls short.
- **Ideas** for gifts and small upgrades that don't cost much.

## How finds are picked

A find makes the list when it solves a real everyday problem, is well made for its price, and is a little bit delightful. There are no paid placements.

## Good to know

As an Amazon Associate, Raccoon Hub earns from qualifying purchases. That never changes which finds are picked.
